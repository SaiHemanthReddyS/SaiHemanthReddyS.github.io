I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Georgia State University, advised by Prof. Ashwin Ashok in the MORSE Studio.

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision and mobile sensing. A major focus is cricket sports analytics, including vision pipelines for detecting and analyzing cracks in cricket pitches and analyzing match video. I also work on optical wireless systems and the data analysis behind them.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a full-stack developer at Zangle Technologies (2023–2025). I hold an MS from New England College and a BTech from JNTUH, Hyderabad.

Outside research, I play cricket in the Atlanta Cricket League, and I trained at Hercules Aviation Academy, where I cleared the DGCA commercial pilot ground examinations.
